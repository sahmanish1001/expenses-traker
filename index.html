What is this

Kharchā is a fast, no-fuss personal expense tracker that runs entirely in your browser. No servers, no ads, no clutter — just a quick way to log where your money goes, with statement and SMS imports that auto-categorize for you, and a Home screen you can arrange however you like.

Data & privacy

All transaction data is stored locally in your browser, scoped to your signed-in Google account — nothing is ever sent to a server. That includes Paste SMS: the text you paste is parsed entirely in your browser and never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data or switching devices won't carry your data over unless you export/import a backup manually from Backup & Sync.

Privacy Policy

📱

What stays on your device

Kharchā is built local-first. Your transactions, balances, loans, and budgets are always kept in a private copy on this device, so the app keeps working — and stays visible only to you — even offline.

👤

Your account & sync

Signing in with Google lets your data follow you across devices. It's stored under your own private row in our database, protected so that only your signed-in account can ever read or write it — nobody else, including other users, can see it.

💬

AI-assisted statement scanning

"Open Claude to scan a statement" opens a separate Claude chat in a new tab so it can read a screenshot you choose to upload there — Kharchā itself never uploads your photos anywhere. You paste the resulting JSON back in yourself.

☁️

Optional Google Drive backup

If you turn on Drive sync, a backup file is written to a private, app-only space in your Drive that no other app (and no one browsing your Drive) can see. It's entirely optional and off by default.

🗑️

Your control

Export a full backup or a CSV/PDF statement anytime from Settings, or clear all your data with one tap. Signing out removes your session from this device; deleting your account removes your data from our servers.

Trust & safety

Security Architecture

🔑

Authentication

Sign-in runs on Google's own OAuth flow — Kharchā never sees or stores your Google password. The signed ID token Google issues is what proves who you are.

🧱

Per-account data isolation

Your data lives in its own database row, locked down with Row Level Security so only your authenticated account can read or write it — this is enforced by the database itself, not just the app's UI.

📴

Local-first fallback

A per-device copy of your data always stays in this browser's local storage as an offline cache — so a patchy connection never means lost work, and the app keeps functioning without a network round-trip.

🏠

Shared rooms

Roommate splitting only ever shares the room-specific fields (shared expenses, balances, rent) with people you've explicitly invited by email — your personal transactions, loans, and budgets never leave your own private data.

🚫

No ads, no data selling

Kharchā doesn't run ads and doesn't sell or share your financial data with advertisers or third parties.

Nepali calendar

Bikram Sambat Engine

🔄

Automatic AD ↔ BS conversion

Every date you enter in the worldwide (AD) calendar is converted to the Nepali Bikram Sambat date automatically — no manual entry needed. You can flip which calendar leads in the transaction list from Settings → Calendar.

📆

Spending grouped by Nepali month

Budgets, the month-over-month comparison in Insights, and the Nepali-month pie chart on Home all group your transactions by real BS months (Baisakh through Chaitra) — the way spending is actually thought about in Nepal.

🏠

BS-based due dates

Room rent and recurring bills are set by a day of the Nepali month (e.g. "1 Bhadra"), and the engine rolls each due date forward to the next BS month automatically once it's passed.

ℹ️

Accuracy note

Conversions use the established month-start dates for the current era (roughly BS 2075–2091 / AD 2018–2035). Month and year are always reliable; the day-of-month can occasionally be off by one right at a month boundary.
